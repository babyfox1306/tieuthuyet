Watch that Direction

Reviewed at: 76. Quite Dangerous
by Carson6319 5/22/2020, 11:42 AM

LivingSpoons has clearly read alot of Xianxia and has decided to use that experience to create this story. Its is one based in a Xianxia world but with a premise that aims to undermine the troupes. 

First off, bloody brilliant premise. The idea of a story from the perspective of a characther acting as the master for generic MC characthers is something I have never seen before. Personally I recon the idea could be used to create a whole new type of story. I mean who wouldnt want to hear Uncle Iroh's perspective. 

This premise alone plus the solid grammer, and ok characthers it enough in my mind to make it a slightly above average read. 

What in my mind stops this story from being truly excellent, top of thie line, is that the premis could have allowed for some Konosuba like shenanigans but it has not gone that way. Instead, LivingSpoons has taken the story in the direction of the MC becoming more amoral and increasingly powerful just in a slightly different way then normal. Rather then this being a comedy, it is increasingly becoming a serious story where the comedy really only comes from missunderstanding being major driving plot points and the fact that the MC is aware that there are other people in his environment who fit the MC archetype whom he can connect with to his advantage. 

As a result this story is starting more and more to feel like a slightly funnier then normal Xianxia. As such if you dont like Xianxia you may find yourself increasingly glassing over as you experience: Cultivation, OP Characthers from higher planes getting in the MC's Face, Full Names Only, and A convoluted ranking system (it goes core, transedent, ancient, immortal right?) 

I hope that I am wrong with my gut feeling about this stories direction. Not gonna know until we see more from LivingSpoon. Hope they come back from Hiatus soon as the work has so far been enjoyable despite the troupes.

The Best Of Xianxia

Reviewed at: 161. Birth of a Collector
by Notsae 12/26/2024, 12:10 PM

I reread this story once a year, always hoping there'll be new chapters each time. Each time I find new things to love, new things I missed or forgot. This is by far and away the best Xianxia story I've ever read, and one of the beat stories period as well. It's truly a unique master piece, a gem sitting alone in a dark and dusty corner. I've never seen anything like it, not before it came out and not since; even amongst those clearly inspired by this magnificent work, none have ever managed to truly match what makes it so great. Funny and serious im equal measure when necessary, with a paranoid protagonist that reminds me of Caiaphus Cain more than anyone and a side cast full of memorable personalities, this story truly stands a cut above allnother Xianxia, both serious and silly alike. My only complaint is that every time I reread it, whenever I hit that last chapter and tap that faded out button, a qave of despair overtakes me that there isn't more to read. I truly hope the author is doing well, and that he returns to continue this story as promised on day. Merry Christmas, to anyone reading this, and may hope burn eternal.
