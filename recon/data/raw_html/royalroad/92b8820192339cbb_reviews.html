What is my 1000-year-old monster self to do when I’ve failed my immortal ascension? Time travel — accidentally — and wind up back in my 9-year-old body, needing to re-cultivate, of course. But after that? Get REVENGE! Muahahahaha! 

But the idiot who killed me — also accidentally — was the main character of the dumb harem Xianxia I transmigrated into. But wait. Wasn’t he only 7 or 8 at this point in time? Well, since I can’t harm a child or the main character without suffering in some way, then I’ll just have to raise him better, faster, stronger, and loyal! Muahahahaha!

This is a Xianxia comedy and satire by the author of Tanking Hits and Tome of Stealth. But it is also a series of interconnected and chronological short stories (featuring the same main characters). For example, it’s similar to the structure of Fred the Vampire Accountant series by the wonderful Drew Hayes.

Updates Every Friday (2:15PM PST). (Currently on Hiatus until July 24th, 2025)

Cover art created with MidJourney and edited by the author. Typography by the author.

Other art within the story ranges from original author created art, commissioned art, edited stock photos and edited AI art. If you're curious about a specific image, just ask the author and they'll let you know.

If you're curious about why there is only one chapter out a week, that is berceuse the author spends 10 hours over three or four days editing each chapter, ensuring the quality and humor. These hours are mentally exhausting. Comedy is hard.

Over the years the author has realized that some people do not understand what satire is. There is no shame in that since it’s more complex than parody. Put over-simply, it’s a genre meant to criticize and poke fun at things but isn’t always funny. Sometimes it even goes over people’s heads.

You can think of it as Sarcasm the Genre.

Quick Review of an excellent Cultivation novel

Reviewed at: Story 10 - The Spy Who Rocked Too Hard (Part 31🎸)
by Tech Cleric Neodymium 7/28/2024, 8:40 AM

This is an excellent novel. Doubly so if you've been somewhat interested in the Cultivation genre, but have found it difficult to try it out due to established tropes, assumed knowledge, and unlikable characters.

This novel manages to keep the elements of the genre that keep it distinct while also adding that fusion twist that many Western authors try to do. Every other one I've seen has either failed to keep that distinctness from other genres, or are just a complete mess. This one manages it.

I might as well go a bit more orderly for the subsections, so let's start with the simplest one, Grammar.

Grammar: It's perfectly readable. No complaints. Very well edited. I've never felt confused while reading. Well, never due to the grammar and not say, as part of a story mystery.

Style: Very good feel to it. You certainly feel the different perspectives of the characters. It's all in first-person and well done. The word choice is carefully selected to ensure the scenes are clear, and to convey the feelings of the characters.

Story Score: Excellent story. Unlike so many other cultivation novels, the main character earns the right to be powerful. Due to some time travel shenanigans, they spent about a thousand years scraping by and forging their own path. No "System" or "Chosen by Heaven's Lottery Scratch Card 1 in 1 Quadrillion Billion Trillion Awesome Destiny" or so many other devices. Nope, just good old style Scientific Theory and a near manic desire to experiment with the rules of the world.

Characters: Love the main character. Hits that right spot of pragmatic, moral, and flawed while still being awesome that makes a character likable. You and I may see that she's wrong in a situation, or has their own prejudices, but it's in a believable and a good way.

Gendo Ikari Approves (not a review swap)

Reviewed at: Story 8 - Tribulation Troubles (Part 2 🎉)
by MDW 10/1/2022, 1:29 PM

I'm only sorry that I binged it too fast. Perhaps I should binge again. If only there was a way to get more chapters faster... (Stares at something in the page).

This story has everything a xianxia should have. Arrogance. Couting death. Absurdity. Crazy alchemical ingredients. Fights. Ridiculous battle moves with ridiculous names. Not only but TWO ridiculously OP Xianxia protagonists. Meta sass at the genre that don't break the fourth wall. Humor.

It makes me envious. Five stars, not a review swap. I don't do review swaps.

It is a rare find. A story that can serve to steal ideas from as a source of inspiration, I mean. (sips tea). It is memorable, and some sections are fine examples of the Dao of Storytelling.

 

So, let's go into the detailed ratings.



Style: It is ridiculously delicious. I might have use the ridiculous family of words too much, but give my mushy brain some respite. Genre tropes are included, dismantled, scorned, then turned upside down. It is not pretentious while having a very pretentious MC.

Story: Awesome mix of time-loop (only once), isekai, xianxia, and humor. It flows well, it skips the boring parts without poking holes in the flow. When a cliché is used, it is with intent to deconstruct it.

Grammar: Perfect. Perhaps some may disagree, but I found no flaws whatsoever. Word choice is nice and well-thought, and I really found nothing to nitpick at.

Character: Can I give a sixth star? Even the support / joke characters are tridimensional. The disposable, throwaway mooks are memorable. The dynamic between the two MC are sweet.

 

----

 

My only problem is with the update schedule. I need MORE!
